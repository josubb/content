Performance value vs task value

Human voice brings emotional complexity that producers pay to keep. AI cannot fully replicate that human nuance.

AI handles repetitive, time-consuming tasks that do not need human feeling. Use AI where nuance is not required.

The error most frequent at this point is assuming every job will accept a synthetic voice. Many publishers still require human narration for flagship audiobooks.

Trackable metric to test: measure hours recorded versus hours edited for one project before and after AI use.

Cost, learning curve and licensing

Adoption needs upfront costs like subscriptions and training time. Expect more editing work at first.

Expect to spend two to six weeks learning prompts and tool settings and to do hands-on practice until results feel repeatable.
