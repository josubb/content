Step 1: establish eligibility & evidence

Confirm statutory factors and gather strong proof of separation. Courts weigh needs, incomes, marriage length, age, health, employability, custody, homemaking, misconduct, and enforceable agreements.

Residency and separation proof

A petitioner proves separation with distinct residences or a clear end to shared domestic life. Acceptable proof includes separate leases, utility bills, and mail at different addresses.

Financial evidence list

A petitioner shows need with bank statements, paystubs, tax returns, and proof of independent bills. The respondent's ability to pay appears in paystubs, W-2s, 1099s, and business records.
