What makes equity marital in Nebraska

An award is marital to the extent it was earned during the marriage and not shown to be separate by agreement. The court examines grant terms, vesting schedules, and any prenup or postnup provisions. Evidence of commingling or transmutation will turn separate holdings into marital assets.
