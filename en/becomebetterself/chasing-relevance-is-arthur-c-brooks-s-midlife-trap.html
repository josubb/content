Midlife can feel unsettling when the sources of past success no longer provide the same sense of purpose. Arthur C. Brooks frames this transition not as decline, but as a necessary recalibration of how value is measured.

From Strength to Strength is worth reading if midlife unease stems from lost professional relevance. Brooks offers a grounded reframe for ambitious people whose careers shaped their identity. Some prescriptions are more aspirational than evidence-based.

From Strength to Strength by Arthur C. Brooks argues that midlife decline need not reduce your value. You can shift from achievement-driven striving toward wisdom, service, relationships, and deeper purpose.

Is From Strength to Strength worth reading at midlife?

From Strength to Strength: Finding Success, Happiness, and Deep Purpose in the Second Half of Life is worth reading if career success still matters. It is also useful when success no longer feels like enough.

The striver’s curse explained

Brooks calls this the striver’s curse. It means chasing the next win while fearing that a lost rank will make your life seem less valuable. His answer is not to abandon success. It is to place success beside relationships, service, and transcendent meaning.

Professional identity can become too narrow. A respected leader may feel empty when nobody needs an answer. The book asks, “What would still make my life matter if my title disappeared?”

The book works best as a sequence, not a set of inspiring ideas.
