Why your garden bird feeder stand falls over

garden bird feeder stands | bird feeding stations | heavy duty feeder poles | patio bird feeder bases | squirrel baffles

Source: https://gardenbirdsguide.co.uk/why-your-garden-bird-feeder-stand-falls-over/
A leaning bird feeder stand after rain can quickly turn a welcoming garden feature into a frustrating hazard. Saturated soil, uneven loading and exposed positioning commonly reduce its stability.

A garden bird feeder stand must support the combined weight of full feeders, water and wildlife without leaning, twisting or falling. Choose it for your ground, full load and squirrel pressure rather than its number of hooks.

Choose a feeder stand by ground and full load

Match the pole, fixing and load limit to your surface: ground-spiked stations suit firm soil, while weighted bases suit level patios.

Match the stand to your surface

A ground spike works best in firm, undisturbed soil; use a weighted base on paving, decking or gravel.
