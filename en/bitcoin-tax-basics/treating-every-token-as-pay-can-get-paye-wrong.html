Treating every token as pay can get PAYE wrong

UK crypto payroll | PAYE compliance | employer National Insurance | crypto reward policies

Source: https://mindyourownbusiness.uk/treating-every-token-as-pay-can-get-paye-wrong/
Crypto rewards can feel like a modern benefit, but HMRC still expects taxable pay to be calculated in GBP. Treating every token transfer as salary can distort PAYE, NIC, and the audit trail required to support the payroll position.

Can a UK startup pay crypto rewards legally?

A UK startup can pay crypto rewards if it meets employment and tax duties. Process the reward in GBP through PAYE, report RTI, and keep proof of value and transfer. But the real risk is poor records or unfunded tax, not the token itself.

Employment income is pay or a reward received because someone is employed. A monthly salary, sales commission, or performance bonus paid in cryptoassets will usually count as employment income. This follows the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003.

A practical structure sets a cash bonus first. The employer runs it through payroll, then converts the net amount to crypto. Think of it as a normal bonus spent on Bitcoin straight after payday.

In this structure, the employer arranges the purchase and transfer.

Which crypto rewards affect your PAYE bill?

The reason for a token reward decides its PAYE treatment and the proof you need. The token label does not decide the tax result.
