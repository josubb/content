Why TV & film production small crews miss kit and data cover

Small TV and film crews operate under tight schedules with costly cameras and irreplaceable footage at stake. They need both physical production insurance and cyber/data protection — one‑day combined cover typically costs £60–£350, while annual packages run £500–£3,000.

One-day shoots often end with missing kit, lost client files or a suspicious payment. Many owners, producers and freelance crew lack in-house IT or security. They worry about theft, data loss and costly delays.

Decision guide for short shoots and annual cover

A short-term policy fits jobs under five shoot days when kit on site is under £25,000. An annual package suits crews with more than ten shoot days a year or kit over £25,000. Add cyber cover when the crew handles client personal data.

When short-term cover fits best

Choose short-term cover for single projects that last one to four days. Short policies list the shoot dates and the kit on site. They cost less up front. A one-day combined public liability and kit policy usually costs between £60 and £350.

When an annual package saves money

Buy annual cover when shoots happen often or owned kit exceeds £25,000. Annual policies remove repeated admin and lower the per-day cost for frequent work. Expect an annual combined production plus basic cyber policy between £500 and £3,000.

A short reminder: check planned shoot days before buying cover.
