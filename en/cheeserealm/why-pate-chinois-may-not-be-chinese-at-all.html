Why pâté chinois may not be Chinese at all

Québécois cuisine | Canadian comfort food | potato bakes

Source: https://cheeserealm.com/why-pate-chinois-may-not-be-chinese-at-all/
A name can mislead before the first bite, especially when it suggests a cuisine the dish does not belong to. Pâté chinois is a Québec baked comfort food of beef, corn, and potato, with an origin story that remains unresolved.

Pâté chinois is Québec’s comforting three-layer bake of seasoned minced beef, sweetcorn and mashed potato—not a Chinese pâté.

Pâté chinois has three defining layers

It is made in a fixed order: seasoned ground beef on the bottom, corn in the middle and mashed potato on top.

Corn is not an optional garnish

Traditional versions use whole-kernel sweetcorn, creamed corn or both; the corn layer is what distinguishes pâté chinois from other beef-and-potato bakes.
