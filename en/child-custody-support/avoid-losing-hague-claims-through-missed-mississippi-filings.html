Summary of process

This overview lists core steps to secure a return and emergency relief in Mississippi.

1. Contact the U.S. Central Authority and local counsel. 2. Preserve travel, school, medical, and communications evidence. 3. File a return petition in state chancery or family court, or federal court under ICARA. 4. Request provisional relief such as a TRO, temporary custody, or passport hold. 5. Coordinate service and international transmission through Central Authorities.

Step 1: immediate emergency actions

The left-behind parent must act now to preserve remedies and evidence.

Contact central authority

The U.S. Department of State Office of Children's Issues is the U.S. Central Authority.

Report the removal online on the Office of Children's Issues web page.

Preserve evidence

Collect passport copies, boarding passes, and travel bookings right away.

Save screenshots of texts, emails, and social posts in a secure folder.

Keep originals where possible and make several copies.

Seek provisional relief

Ask a court for a temporary restraining order or temporary custody order.
