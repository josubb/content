Fast access to cash can matter when unexpected costs arise or savings plans change. Premium Bonds and easy-access ISAs offer different withdrawal routes, processing times, and potential delays that affect when funds reach your bank account.

Key takeaways: speed, typical timings and practical priorities

- Premium Bonds withdrawals normally take 2–4 working days from an online request to cleared funds in a bank account; postal requests and manual checks add time. - Easy-access ISAs can be faster, but vary by provider, some offer instant or same-day payments to a verified account, while others typically take 1–3 working days. - Weekends, bank holidays, verification and large amounts add delay; working-day counts exclude weekends and UK bank holidays. - Transfers between ISAs usually take longer than withdrawals (commonly 5–30 working days) and can create short-term liquidity issues despite preserving tax benefits. - Practical priority: verify bank details and keep a small balance in a truly instant-access account for emergencies; treat Premium Bonds as liquid but not immediate cash.

How quickly can you withdraw from Premium Bonds?

NS&I (National Savings & Investments) manages Premium Bonds and publishes practical guidance on encashment. The typical flow when requesting to cash in Premium Bonds online is: request acknowledged by NS&I, internal processing (usually 1–2 working days), and a payment sent via Faster Payments or Bacs to the nominated bank account (which may add 0–2 working days depending on the bank and payment method). That yields an indicative range of 2–4 working days for most online requests. Postal or telephone requests often add processing time.
