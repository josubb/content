Protecting veterinary practices: cyber insurance explained

Cyber insurance for veterinary practices | veterinary cyber insurance | data breach veterinary | ransomware cover vet | SME cyber insurance

Source: https://dealergen.uk/cyber-insurance-for-veterinary-practices/
Veterinary practices depend on connected systems to protect animal welfare, client trust and daily operations. A cyber incident can quickly disrupt appointments, expose sensitive data and create significant financial and regulatory pressure.

Is the veterinary practice responsible if a client’s pet records are leaked? Is there cover if a booking system is locked by ransomware? For many small UK clinics the answers are unclear and the risk is growing.

This guide explains cyber insurance for veterinary practices in plain British English, focused on practical decisions rather than jargon. It sets out what policies typically cover, how to read policy wording, how to assess risk in a clinic, what happens during a claim, and how GDPR affects premiums and insurer selection. The content is neutral and educational; decisions about cover should involve regulated advisers.
