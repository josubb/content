Don't mistake 18×18×24 for an adult crested gecko tank

Source: https://reptilevivarium.co.uk/don-t-mistake-18-18-24-for-an-adult-crested-gecko-tank/
An 18×18×24 enclosure may look tall, but it can limit an adult crested gecko’s usable climbing space. For long-term housing, an 18×18×36 setup provides the vertical structure needed for more natural movement and enrichment.

It is easy to mistake an 18 × 18 × 24 inch terrarium for a proper adult home because many retailers still label it “adult size”. At 60 cm tall, though, it quickly becomes crowded once you add safe branches, cover, feeding ledges and the clear routes a crested gecko needs to climb, rest and feel secure.

An adult crested gecko tank size should be a tall, front-opening 45 × 45 × 90 cm (18 × 18 × 36 inch) enclosure for one gecko. The older 45 × 45 × 60 cm size is a bare minimum rather than the welfare-first target.

Choose 45 × 45 × 90 cm for an adult gecko

For one adult crested gecko, choose a 45 × 45 × 90 cm vertical enclosure as the long-term home.

A crested gecko, or Correlophus ciliatus, is arboreal. Arboreal means it spends much of its life off the ground, much like a person choosing stairs and landings rather than sitting on a bare floor. It comes from the vegetation of New Caledonia, so a tall enclosure suits its natural way of moving.

Buy once, if possible: 45 × 45 × 90 cm is the preferred enclosure for one adult.
