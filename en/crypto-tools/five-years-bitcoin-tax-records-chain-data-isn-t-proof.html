Bitcoin tax data retention & chain analysis

For most individual taxpayers, keep records for at least five years from 31 January after the relevant tax year.

Keep records longer if HMRC opens a compliance check. Do the same if you correct a return or claim an allowable loss. Keep them while assets from earlier years remain in your pool.

Records that support a tax calculation

Exchange CSV exports: trades, order history, deposits, withdrawals, and trading fees from Coinbase, Kraken, Binance, or another platform. Wallet evidence: wallet labels, public addresses, transaction IDs (TXIDs), dates, quantities, and proof of both sides of an internal transfer. GBP valuation records: the sterling value at each purchase, disposal, swap, reward, or fee payment. Funding evidence: bank statements, card confirmations, or payment-provider records that explain fiat purchases. Calculation files: records showing same-day matching, the 30-day bed and breakfast rule, and Section 104 pooling.

Your evidence should explain each number on your return.
