Automated MSP operations can strengthen security controls, reduce response times and improve ransomware resilience for UK SMEs. However, automation also changes the evidence, accountability and risk posture assessed during cyber insurance underwriting and claims.

Hammer Distribution and Acronis: why this matters beyond the channel

The partnership between Hammer Distribution and Acronis, reported by IT Pro, focuses on automated cyber operations for managed service providers (MSPs). At first glance, this may sound like a channel-industry announcement with limited relevance to a small or medium-sized business. In practice, it reflects a significant shift in how many UK SMEs will buy, operate and evidence their cyber security.

Rather than employing a full internal security team, many SMEs rely on an MSP for Microsoft 365 administration, endpoint management, backups, patching and helpdesk support. Automation can bring these functions together and make them more consistent. That has direct implications for cyber insurance because insurers increasingly assess not only whether an organisation has security controls, but whether those controls are actively managed, monitored and recoverable when an incident occurs.

For a UK SME, the important question is not whether Hammer or Acronis is the right supplier in every case. The more useful question is: can your IT provider demonstrate that your essential cyber controls work, are reviewed and will support an effective recovery?

What automated cyber operations can mean for an SME

Automated cyber operations generally refer to technology and processes that reduce manual work in detecting threats, applying updates, responding to alerts and protecting data. Depending on the MSP’s service design, this can include:

- automated endpoint protection and alerting; - vulnerability and patch management; - backup monitoring and recovery testing; - detection of suspicious account activity; - centralised reporting across devices, users and cloud services; - automated isolation of a compromised device; and - workflows that create tickets and escalate high-risk incidents.

These capabilities can reduce the period between a threat appearing and an organisation taking action.

*The decisive factor may be not what is automated, but what happens when automation fails...*
