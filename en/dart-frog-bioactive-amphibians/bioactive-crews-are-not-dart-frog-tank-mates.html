Bioactive Crews Are Not Dart Frog Tank Mates

Dart frogs require tank mates that support a stable microhabitat without adding predation, stress, or competition. Springtails and carefully selected isopods function as clean-up crews, not cohabiting display animals.

A busy, planted vivarium can look like a miniature rainforest, but adding more animals does not make it healthier. Most dart frog tank mates should not include geckos, reptiles or other vertebrates; the safest additions are springtails and suitable isopods, while frog cohabitation requires species-specific planning.

What can safely share a dart frog vivarium?

A bioactive vivarium uses organisms such as springtails and isopods to break down waste. They form a cleaning team in the soil, rather than vertebrate pets sharing the frogs’ space.
