Dating outcomes narrow when one channel is expected to provide both reach and meaningful repeated contact. Apps expand access, while serendipity creates context; relying on either alone leaves part of the dating pool untouched.

Creating serendipity vs. using dating apps is a false choice. Apps expand your reach. Real-world routines create repeated, lower-pressure chances to connect.
