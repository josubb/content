Film productions depend on tightly controlled rights, releases, and image usage from the first day on set. In North Carolina, a film permit may authorize production activity, but it does not automatically license commercial still photographs.

A North Carolina filming permit can allow work at a location. It does not clear the still images made there.

Recognizable talent, private property, logos, artwork, and products may need separate releases. They may also require commercial-use rights before images reach a client deck, ad buy, or social launch.
