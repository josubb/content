How demand looks across MA

State data and job boards show steady openings for special education roles. Urban districts and some rural systems report persistent vacancies.

The NEA reported a Massachusetts average teacher salary of $91,815 for 2021-22. That figure includes all certified positions and senior teachers. Do not use it to estimate entry SPED pay.

What to measure for ROI

Add tuition, program fees, MTEL costs, DESE fees, and lost wages into your model. Also include health care and pension value for a fuller picture.

Estimate employer tuition aid and any signing bonuses before you calculate net cost. That changes breakeven years sharply.
