Wyoming sports jobs need proof beyond a BS

A Bachelor of Science in Sports Management is a useful starting credential in Wyoming. It is not a hiring guarantee. Employers often want a degree plus game-day work, internships, ticketing software, event work, or sales results.

Think of the degree as a driver's permit. It shows that you learned the rules, but employers need proof that you can drive in traffic.

The degree clears only one gate
