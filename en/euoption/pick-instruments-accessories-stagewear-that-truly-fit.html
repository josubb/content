Decision factors for buying gear

Choose equipment by role, venue and maintenance cost, not by logo or lowest price. Buyers should prioritise playability first, serviceability second, then aesthetics. Consumer protection and safety standards affect buying and returns in the UK.

Which specs change playability

Scale length, action height and nut width directly change how an instrument feels. Measure scale length in millimetres and check nut width in mm before ordering strings or cases. A mismatched scale or nut causes poor action and intonation.

Warranty and legal protections to check

The Consumer Rights Act 2015 gives a statutory right to reject faulty goods within 30 days in England.

- Check for UKCA marking for products placed on the Great Britain market. CE marking applies for EU conformity. - When buying in the UK, prioritise UKCA or seller declarations of conformity.
