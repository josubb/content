Step 1: build a 30-day sprint

Start the 30-day sprint to produce measurable outreach outcomes quickly.

Design one time-boxed plan for 30 days that balances studio work and outreach. Set weekly targets: five tailored contacts, two visibility actions, and three portfolio updates. Expect this step to take 60 to 90 minutes daily on average.

The most common error is underestimating admin time for tailored messages.

Sprint daily blocks

Divide each day into three fixed blocks. Block one: 30 to 60 minutes for studio work. Block two: 15 to 30 minutes for visibility. Block three: 15 to 30 minutes for outreach prep.

Depending on the day, this totals roughly 60 to 120 minutes. Plan for lighter days near 60 to 90 minutes. Reserve longer sessions up to 120 minutes for deep production days.

This layout clarifies expected daily time and helps prevent underestimating admin time for tailored messages. Stick to the blocks for at least 21 out of 30 days to form a habit that produces results.
