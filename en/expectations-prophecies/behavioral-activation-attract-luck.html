Behavioral Activation to Attract Luck is a practical behavior plan that schedules more opportunity-seeking actions and tracks outcomes to test what raises chance events. It serves people who want measurable, study-backed ways to increase opportunity.

Process summary

In the context of Behavioral Activation to Attract Luck, run a short, repeatable experiment. Set a 3-4 week window for a clear before-and-after comparison. Keep the plan simple so it is doable.

1. Define baseline activities and metrics in one session. 2. Schedule specific opportunity-seeking actions twice weekly or daily. 3. Track objective outcomes and perceived luck daily. 4. Compare conversion rates after 2-4 weeks and iterate.

Behavioral Activation to Attract Luck model

In the context of the model, the goal is increased exposure plus better decisions. Exposure means more contacts, applications, or attempts. Decision quality improves when mood and energy rise. Studies of BA for mood show mechanisms that raise action rates and decision clarity (Ekers et al.; later reviews).

Step 1 Define baseline and goals

Measure current activity for seven days to establish a baseline. Collect objective counts like contacts made, emails sent, or events attended. Also record a daily 0-10 perceived luck rating.
