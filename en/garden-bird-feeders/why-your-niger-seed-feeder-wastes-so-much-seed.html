Why Your Niger Seed Feeder Wastes So Much Seed

Niger seed feeders | Goldfinch feeding | Bird feeder hygiene

Source: https://gardenbirdsguide.co.uk/why-your-niger-seed-feeder-wastes-so-much-seed/
Niger seed is small, expensive, and easily lost when feeder ports are too wide or poorly designed. Fine feeding ports reduce waste while giving goldfinches comfortable access in an English garden.

A good nyjer seed feeder has narrow, smoothly finished ports that goldfinches can use without pouring expensive seed below. Choose a feeder for the seed size, then place and maintain it so spills stay manageable.

Pick by port fit, not the nyjer label

Choose a specialist feeder with fine feeding ports: small, rounded holes that hold seed in place until a bird picks it out. Niger and nyjer are names for the same small seed in UK searches and listings, but neither name guarantees that a tube will stop spills.
