News of avian flu can make every garden feeder feel like a potential risk. Careful hygiene, reduced crowding, and current Defra guidance help protect wild birds without abruptly removing a vital food source.

A local avian flu alert can be worrying, especially if wild birds, pets or poultry share your garden. Wild-bird-only gardens do not automatically need feeders removed, but poultry and captive birds need stricter separation and biosecurity.

Decide by the birds you keep, not the headline

Your action depends on whether the garden has wild visitors only, poultry, or captive pet birds.
