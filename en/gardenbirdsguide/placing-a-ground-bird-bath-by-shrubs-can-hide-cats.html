Placing a Ground Bird Bath by Shrubs Can Hide Cats

ground bird baths | garden bird water | cat-safe bird feeding

Source: https://gardenbirdsguide.co.uk/placing-a-ground-bird-bath-by-shrubs-can-hide-cats/
A ground bird bath can offer birds a refreshing refuge, but placement near shrubs may conceal stalking cats. Maintain a safe working depth of 5 cm and provide a sloping, grippy exit to reduce risk.

A ground bird bath can suit robins, blackbirds, dunnocks and other birds that naturally forage at soil level. Place it where they have clear views in all directions, on firm ground that drains well, rather than beside dense shrubs or tall cover where a cat could wait unseen.

A safe ground bath needs open views and shallow water

A ground-level dish is safe when birds can spot danger, stand securely and leave the water quickly.

Choose ground level for ground-feeding birds

A practical size is between 30 and 45 cm across. That gives several small birds room without creating a large, hard-to-clean pool. Choose a heavy terracotta saucer, rough stone dish or textured plastic bowl with a wide base.

Keep cover close, but not cat-close

A bath needs escape cover within roughly 2 to 4 metres, such as a small tree, open shrub or hedge edge. It should not sit directly beside dense laurel, bramble or a thick conifer. That gap gives birds a clear view while still allowing a fast flight to safety.

A ground bird bath is a shallow dish placed at soil level, designed to suit ground-feeding birds that are already comfortable foraging on lawns, paths and borders. Robins, blackbirds, dunnocks and thrushes may favour this type of garden bird water source because they can walk straight to the rim rather than fly up to a pedestal. Choose it when your bird bath placement can provide open sightlines, firm drainage and nearby escape cover.
