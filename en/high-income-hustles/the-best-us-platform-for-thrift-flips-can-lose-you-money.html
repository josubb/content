Every pound matters when turning second-hand finds into profit. Fees, postage, return policies and selling speed can determine whether a thrift flip succeeds or stalls.

Choosing the wrong marketplace can turn a promising thrift flip into a low-margin sale or a loss. Compare net profit after fees, shipping, returns, and selling time, not just the sale price.

Choose a platform by item, price, and speed

Choose based on category, expected sale price, package size, buyer audience, and return risk.

Check sold comps before listing

Check sold comps, meaning completed listings that buyers actually paid for. Active listings show hopes. Sold listings show demand and condition details, while unsold comparable listings show the competition.

Start on eBay when unsure

eBay is the safest default for shoes, electronics, tools, games, collectibles, cameras, and known brands. In many categories, seller charges are about 13.6% plus $0.40 per order. Rates vary by category.

Promoted Listings can add roughly 2% to 10% or more. Choose eBay first if you sell mixed inventory or searchable items.

Calculate net profit before you list anything

A thrift flip works only when the final payout covers sourcing, cleaning, packing, shipping, fees, refunds, and taxes.
