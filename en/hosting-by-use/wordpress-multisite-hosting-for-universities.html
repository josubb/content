Key takeaways: WordPress multisite hosting for universities in 60 seconds

- Multisite reduces operational overhead by centralizing themes and plugins, but only if governance, role separation, and tenant isolation are designed up front. - Choose hosting with SAML/Shibboleth and InCommon support for seamless campus SSO; integrations fail on low-tier shared hosts. - Performance planning must include peak benchmarks (enrollment, course launches); scale with object caching, Redis, and autoscaling application tiers to avoid outages. - Compliance and data residency matter: FERPA, GDPR, and institutional policies can eliminate some cloud regions or providers. - Cost model should be per-site or per-peak-usage with headroom for term starts; flat low-cost VPS often becomes more expensive when factoring time and outages.
