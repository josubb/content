A breakup can leave unseen recurring charges that continue to impact your finances and credit. This technical checklist provides step-by-step procedures to discover, stop, transfer, or split recurring billing, secure your credit profile, and collect documentary evidence quickly.

Worried about surprise charges or a credit hit after a breakup? Unexpected recurring billing quickly turns separation into financial stress. Joint accounts can keep charging and evidence may disappear if accounts are not audited.

This guide shows how to manage joint subscriptions and recurring billing with a clear, calm plan. It explains how to find, transfer, cancel, or split subscriptions and stop recurring charges.

Summary of the process

This section lists the exact steps to complete the process quickly. Read the numbered list and start the top three items immediately.

1. Run a full audit of accounts and transactions to identify every recurring charge. Capture receipts and timestamps for each item. 2. Stop or transfer subscriptions at the provider level. Then block payments at the bank or card issuer. Use both tracks for reliability. 3. Send written cancellation or transfer requests and save replies. Keep confirmation numbers and screenshots. 4. File chargebacks or ACH disputes only after collecting evidence and following provider procedures. Banks require documentation. 5. Use the audit CSV and templates below to present evidence during mediation or court.
