A denied payment-fraud claim can instantly expose an online shop to significant financial loss and erode customer trust. This briefing identifies why insurers reject e-commerce payment-fraud claims, common coverage gaps, and practical next steps to secure your operation.

An unauthorised high‑value card loss can wipe out a month’s cash flow for a 10‑person online shop. Many SMEs assume standard cyber cover will refund it.

Decision‑makers face mixed insurer definitions and overlapping products. They also meet costly exclusions that leave losses unpaid and compliance gaps unaddressed.

E‑commerce: cyber insurance vs payment‑fraud protection — if an online shop is at stake, know which losses each covers. Cyber insurance pays for data breaches, ransomware, forensic costs, third‑party claims and business interruption.

Payment fraud protection and anti‑fraud services aim to prevent or refund unauthorised card payments and chargebacks. These services are often excluded from standard cyber policies.
