At 28 mm, tit nest box entrances keep larger birds out

tit nest boxes | blue tit nesting | garden bird homes

Source: https://gardenbirdsguide.co.uk/at-28-mm-tit-nest-box-entrances-keep-larger-birds-out/
A 28 mm entrance hole creates a controlled threshold for small tits seeking a secure nesting site. This dimension supports blue, coal and great tits while reducing access for larger garden birds.

A 28 mm entrance suits great tits while helping to keep out many larger garden birds. If you want blue tits or coal tits, use a smaller 25 mm hole; a 32 mm opening is less selective and may admit larger species.

For reliable tit nest box dimensions, use a 120 × 120 mm internal floor, a 200 mm front, a 250 mm back, and position the entrance hole 125–150 mm above the floor.
