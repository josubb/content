Why your Roamwild feeder still attracts starlings

Roamwild feeders | Squirrel-proof feeding | UK garden birds

Source: https://gardenbirdsguide.co.uk/why-your-roamwild-feeder-still-attracts-starlings/
A feeder can appear squirrel-proof and still be overwhelmed by starlings within hours. Correct hanging height, placement, and seed selection determine whether a Roamwild feeder protects the food intended for smaller garden birds.

A bird feeder model in the UK can reduce losses to squirrels, pigeons and heavier garden visitors, but starlings may still gain access. They are light enough to keep some weight-operated perches open and quickly learn to use nearby perches, cages and wires.

Choose Roamwild by food, pests and garden space

Choose PestOff for repeated squirrel raids, or an Arch Window feeder when close viewing matters more than pest control.

PestOff feeders for squirrel pressure

The PestOff range suits seed feeding where grey squirrels empty standard tube feeders. Use sunflower hearts or a suitable seed mix only where the product instructions confirm compatibility.

Window feeding for close observation

The Roamwild Arch Window feeder suits a clean, flat window for watching robins, tits and finches. It is not a full solution where squirrels can reach the sill or large flocks feed daily.
