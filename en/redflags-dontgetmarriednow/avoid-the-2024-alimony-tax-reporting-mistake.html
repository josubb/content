Why the TCJA changed alimony tax treatment for 2024

The Tax Cuts and Jobs Act of 2017 changed federal tax treatment for spousal support. Agreements executed after Dec. 31, 2018 fall under the new rule.

The result is clear: new agreements made under current law do not give a federal deduction to the payer. The recipient likewise does not report the payment as federal income.

The legal deadline for the TCJA cutoff is Dec. 31, 2018. That date determines whether IRC Section 71 and Section 215 apply or whether the TCJA rule governs.

The error most frequent at this stage is assuming every alimony payment is nondeductible without checking the execution date. The distinction between pre-2019 and post-2018 agreements controls federal treatment and the cash-tax math.
