Divorce reshapes not only family life, but also the financial structure built during the marriage. Understanding how assets are classified and divided is essential to protect liquidity and preserve value.

Comparative quick table

This table shows at a glance how community and equitable rules treat common assets.

Quick overview

Community property presumes most marital assets split 50/50. Equitable distribution divides assets fairly using listed factors.

How to use this table

Use the table to spot which assets matter most for your case. Then focus discovery and tracing on those items.

Asset category Community property (presumed 50/50) Equitable distribution (factors) Wages and bank deposits earned during marriage Treated as marital; split equally unless traced or waived Considered marital; judge weighs earning, need, and contributions Primary home bought during marriage Full marital asset; 50/50 presumption Value divided fairly; offsets possible (home vs retirement) Premarital assets and inheritances Usually separate if kept distinct and not commingled Us
