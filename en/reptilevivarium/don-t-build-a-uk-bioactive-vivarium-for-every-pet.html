A successful UK bioactive vivarium begins with the animal’s thermal, moisture and space requirements—not with a decorative vision. Heat gradients, drainage layers and living substrate must function as a controlled system before any aesthetic choice is added.

Choose the animal before the living system

Choose the species first and rule out unsuitable builds.
