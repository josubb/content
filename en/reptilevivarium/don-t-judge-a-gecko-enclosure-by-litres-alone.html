A gecko enclosure is more than a volume measurement; it must support the animal’s natural movement, thermal needs and security. Species-specific behaviour determines the required floor space, height, ventilation and hiding opportunities.

Gecko enclosure suitability depends on the right shape, usable space, ventilation, heat, humidity and security for each species. Buying the biggest tank is not enough. Leopard geckos need broad floor space. Crested geckos need climbing height. For one adult crested or gargoyle gecko, 45 × 45 × 60 cm is a common starting point.

Choose enclosure shape by gecko species

Choose the species first. Then choose the enclosure orientation. Terrestrial geckos need usable floor space. Arboreal geckos need furnished height and secure climbing routes.
