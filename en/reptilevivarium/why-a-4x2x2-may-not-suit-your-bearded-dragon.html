Why a 4x2x2 May Not Suit Your Bearded Dragon

Bearded dragon vivariums | UVB lighting | Reptile enclosure setup | Reptile welfare UK

Source: https://reptilevivarium.co.uk/why-a-4x2x2-may-not-suit-your-bearded-dragon/
A 120 × 60 × 60 cm enclosure can appear adequate while still limiting essential movement, thermoregulation, and lighting placement. For a bearded dragon, usable floor area and the distance between the basking zone and UVB lamp determine whether those dimensions truly function.

A 4 × 2 × 2 ft vivarium is not automatically suitable for a bearded dragon. Its outside dimensions can hide a smaller usable floor area once you account for wall thickness, substrate depth, door tracks and raised furnishings. A starter kit may still work, but only if its heat and lighting layout remains safe at your dragon’s level.

A suitable bearded dragon vivarium is not judged by litres or outside measurements alone. For most adult dragons, start with at least 120 × 60 × 60 cm of usable internal space, then check that you can provide a safe basking surface, a genuinely cooler retreat and effective UVB at the animal’s height.

Can a 4 × 2 × 2 ft vivarium suit your dragon?

A 4 × 2 × 2 ft enclosure can suit one adult bearded dragon only when the internal floor, UVB distance and heat gradient still work after it is furnished.
