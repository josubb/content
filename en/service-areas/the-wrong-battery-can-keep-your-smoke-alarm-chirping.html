A smoke alarm chirp can feel like a small warning that refuses to be ignored. The correct replacement battery is determined by the alarm’s label and specifications, not by its physical shape alone.

Identify your alarm before buying a battery

Identify the alarm’s power type before buying a battery. A battery drawer usually means a replaceable battery. “Sealed 10-year” means you must replace the whole unit eventually. A hardwired alarm normally uses mains electricity and has battery backup.

Use the three-way alarm check

Look for a battery door, a “sealed lithium” label, or “AC”, “mains-powered”, or “hardwired” wording. Check while the alarm remains on the ceiling. This avoids buying a battery that cannot fit.
