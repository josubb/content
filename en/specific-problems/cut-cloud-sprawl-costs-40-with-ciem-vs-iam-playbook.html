Cloud entitlement management vs IAM for cloud sprawl:

- CIEM complements, not replaces, traditional IAM. - Choose CIEM when you need continuous entitlement discovery, path analysis, risk-based least-privilege, and automated remediation across multi-cloud dynamic workloads. - If your organization is small, single-cloud, and has stable roles, strengthen IAM first. - This article includes a decision checklist and high-level ROI guidance.
