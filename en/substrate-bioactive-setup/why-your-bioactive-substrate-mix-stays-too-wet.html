Why your bioactive substrate mix stays too wet

Source: https://reptilevivarium.co.uk/why-your-bioactive-substrate-mix-stays-too-wet/
A substrate that remains wet can silently destabilize the enclosure, affecting humidity, airflow, and animal comfort. The issue is often not watering frequency, but a mix with too much organic retention and insufficient mineral structure.

Choose by habitat, not a “bioactive” label

Choose substrate for the animal’s natural biome, humidity needs and digging behaviour. Do not choose it because a product is labelled “bioactive”.
