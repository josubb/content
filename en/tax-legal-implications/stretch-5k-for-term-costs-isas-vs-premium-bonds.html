Every pound of term funding needs a clear role when costs can change quickly. A Cash ISA can protect most short-term savings, while Premium Bonds can hold a small, flexible fun pot with prize-based upside.

Students: short answer first. For 1–36 months put the bulk in a Cash ISA. Keep a small fun pot in Premium Bonds only if you can accept low expected returns.
