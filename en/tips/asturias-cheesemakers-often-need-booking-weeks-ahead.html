First, book the right kind of visit

A visit to a cheesemaker in Asturias works best when the booking comes before the drive. Some producers welcome visitors by appointment only. Others sell cheese on site but do not open the production area to the public.

The safest approach is to treat this like booking a small guided activity, not a casual stop. That means checking the day, the hour, the visit format, and whether the producer expects a group or accepts just one family.

The most common mistake is assuming every cheese factory works like a museum. It does not. In rural Asturias, production still comes first, and visitor access fits around milking, making, and aging.
