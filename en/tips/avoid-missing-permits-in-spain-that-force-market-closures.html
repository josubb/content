Summary of the process

Keep a clear checklist before filing your applications.

Market type and initial call

Decide whether the event is a recurring weekly market or a one-off fair. Procedures differ for recurring and one-off events. A periodic market usually needs a market licence or a long-term concession. A one-off event often files as a temporary activity under street-occupancy rules.

The error most frequent here is assuming both follow the same calendar. They do not and deadlines differ.

What to ask the municipal officer

Request the market ordinance, the fee schedule for stalls and the exact forms for public-space occupation. Ask the officer for their email and phone for follow up. Ask which attachments they need: plan of stalls, traffic plan, waste plan and insurance certificate.

A frequent trap is municipal sites listing forms only in the local language. Ask for the PDF directly by email.
