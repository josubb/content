Weekly markets in Spain can hide significant price differences behind similar products and services. A weighted five-factor checklist helps compare vendors objectively, reduce shopping time, and identify genuine value before spending.

Summary of the process

Start with a clear buyer profile, capture normalized prices, record service terms, score vendors, and pick the best option.

The workflow needs two short visits or one visit plus phone checks within seven days.

Apply the scoring sheet to 6–10 representative SKUs and export results to CSV or Excel for tracking.

A short checklist saves time and avoids bad supplier choices.

Define objectives and timeline

Decide what matters most: price, quality, delivery, or invoicing for businesses.

Set a practical timeline: capture data in one market day and verify within three to seven days.

Keep changes small and test them across two market days.

Prepare sample SKUs

Choose 6–10 common items like tomatoes, chicken breast, bread, cheese, olives, and fish.

Standardize units to kg, litre, or unit before recording any price.

Write the unit beside each price to avoid conversion errors.

A short checklist helps teams stay aligned.

Step 1: define buyer profile

Declare the buyer type and priorities before collecting any prices.

A household values price and convenience more than traceability.

A restaurant or shop must weigh quality and service more heavily and require invoices.

Household vs professional weights

For households, use these weights: Price 30%, Quality 30%, Variety 15%, Service 15%, Payment/Security 10%.

For professionals, use: Price 20%, Quality 40%, Variety 15%, Service 20%, Payment 5%.

If you change Payment weight, state the final weights and ensure they sum to 100%.

Practical checklist for buyer needs

Write down weekly quantities, delivery needs, and certification preferences like organic or PDO.

Record whether invoices are required and which payment methods are necessary.

Keep this sheet with the price samples for quick reference.

Step 2: collect prices & samples

Collect five to ten sample prices per SKU across several stalls and market days.
