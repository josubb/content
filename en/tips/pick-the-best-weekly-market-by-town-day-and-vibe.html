Which weekly markets are worth the trip?

The fastest way to choose is simple: match the market to your goal, then check the day and access. A famous name can disappoint if you arrive late or on the wrong weekday.

The best weekly market is the one that fits your trip, not the one with the biggest name. That matters more than people think, especially in Spain, where market days vary a lot by town and region.

A practical rule helps here. Go early for food, go later only if you want a calmer browse, and do not assume every open-air market sells the same thing. Some are farmers' market style, some are flea market driven, and some mix both.
