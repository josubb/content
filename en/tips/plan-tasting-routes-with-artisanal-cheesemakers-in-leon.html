How to choose and visit producers

Choosing the right producer starts with three facts: milk type, visit policy and seasonality.

Most small cheesemakers require an appointment or sell only on market days.

Expect driving times between sites of 40 to 90 minutes on mountain roads.

Confirm dates twice: one week and two days before travel.

Booking and opening hours

Call or email every producer before travel and confirm the visit time.

Ask for preferred days, because many open only on market days or by appointment.

A common mistake is assuming posted hours exist; many farm-based producers do not list times online.

What to ask on arrival

Ask for the batch or affinage date before tasting or buying any cheese.

Request traceability details that follow Regulation (EC) No 2073/2005 for microbiological criteria.

Ask whether cheeses are raw-milk or pasteurised and whether the producer ships abroad.

On-site etiquette and costs

Bring small cash and be ready to pay tasting fees when required.

Tastings at many small producers fall in the €6–€15 per person range. Fees change with group size and tour options.

Respect milking schedules and quiet zones on working farms.
