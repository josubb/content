Setting up at a weekly market in Spain can be stressful when utilities or insurance are uncertain; a last‑minute refusal or fine can wipe out days of work. Verify which markets provide electricity, water and required insurance, and confirm fees, available amperage and booking requirements to avoid penalties.

Need power, water or valid insurance for a stall at a Spanish weekly market?

Many small vendors find that municipal rules, low amperage and absent taps make planning hard. Most weekly markets in Spain do not automatically give electricity, water or insurance. Availability, amperage and fees vary by town and by market. Some markets sell paid power posts or taps. Vendors normally hold public liability insurance.

Plan ahead and get written confirmations before the market day.
