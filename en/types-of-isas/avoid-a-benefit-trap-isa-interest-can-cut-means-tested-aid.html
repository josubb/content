Tax-free interest can still reduce means-tested support if it pushes income above key thresholds. Comparing ISA interest with Premium Bonds prizes helps pensioners estimate the real value of each option before moving savings.

For most pensioners, Cash ISA interest and Premium Bonds prizes are tax‑free. However, means‑tested benefits regard capital holdings, so benefit payments can fall if savings rise. Always model benefit effects before moving large sums.
