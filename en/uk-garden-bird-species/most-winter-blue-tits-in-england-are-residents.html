Blue tits (Cyanistes caeruleus) are familiar English garden birds with blue caps, white cheeks and yellow bellies; most birds seen in winter are local residents rather than arrivals.

Blue tit bird ID: face, belly and feeder clues

A blue tit is best identified by its blue cap, white cheeks and yellow belly seen together, and it often hangs upside down at feeders before darting back to cover.

Blue tit or great tit?

A great tit is larger, with a black head and broad black stripe down its yellow breast, while a blue tit has a blue cap and a finer dark facial line.
