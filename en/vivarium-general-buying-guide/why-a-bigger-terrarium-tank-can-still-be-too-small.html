A 120 cm terrarium may meet the minimum length requirement, yet still restrict an adult bearded dragon’s ability to thermoregulate, climb, and breathe in stable conditions. Usable height, ventilation design, and thermal gradients determine whether the enclosure supports welfare beyond its floor dimensions.

A terrarium tank works only when its size, vents, material and heat layout suit the animal. Buy for adult size and natural behaviour, not the box label. The real risk is buying a tank that looks good but fails daily welfare needs.

Choose by adult species needs, not tank litres

Choose by adult size and behaviour first. Litres show empty volume, not floor length, climbing height or heat gradient.

A heat gradient is a warm end and a cooler end. Think of it like choosing a sunny or shaded seat.
