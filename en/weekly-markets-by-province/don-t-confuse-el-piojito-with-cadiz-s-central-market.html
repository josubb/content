Cádiz markets can look similar at first glance, but each one serves a distinct purpose. Knowing where to find fresh produce, low-cost street goods, or second-hand treasures avoids a wasted trip.

Searching for a Cádiz market can send you to Mercado Central when you meant El Piojito. It can also leave you expecting Sunday flea stalls that are not there. Use this guide to match your day with the right market. Check the latest notice before travelling.

Cádiz market types and usual opening days

Cádiz has a covered food market, a Monday street market, and occasional flea-market events.

Is el piojito mercado central?

No. Mercado Central is in Plaza de la Libertad. It is the place for fish, meat, fruit, vegetables, and prepared food.

El Piojito usually takes place on Mondays near Avenida de la Bahía. It is roughly 25 to 35 minutes from the Cathedral on foot. Its stalls sell low-cost clothes, home goods, and daily items.

They are separate markets with separate purposes.

Which market fits your day?

Choose Mercado Central for fresh food and a central old-town stop. Choose El Piojito for bargains on a Monday morning. Treat any rastro as a separate event until its organiser confirms it.

The most common mistake is matching only the name. A market listing can sound right but refer to a different day or area.
