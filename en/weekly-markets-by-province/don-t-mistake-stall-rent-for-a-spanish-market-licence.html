Don't mistake stall rent for a Spanish market licence

Spain market stall fees | municipal vending licence | weekly market permits

Source: https://weeklymarketsspain.com/don-t-mistake-stall-rent-for-a-spanish-market-licence/
A low stall rent can make a weekly market in Spain look immediately affordable. But €2–€15 per metre usually covers space only, not the licences, insurance, travel, and compliance costs required to trade legally.

A weekly market pitch in Spain can cost from €2–€15 per metre per day. Private rentals may cost €20–€100+ daily, while annual permits often cost €100–€1,000+.

The real issue is not the pitch fee alone. Insurance, travel, tax status, and equipment can turn a €10 fee into a €60–€150 trading day.

What sets the cost of a market pitch?

A Spanish mercadillo has no national tariff. The local Ayuntamiento, or town hall, controls public space.

The tasa municipal por instalación de puestos pays for street or square use. Think of it like paying for a reserved parking bay.

It is not a licence for all Spain.

The town may charge by metre, square metre, day, quarter, or year. Each system changes your final bill.

Meterage changes the published fee

A rate of €3 per metre makes a four-metre pitch cost €12 that day. A €10 rate makes the same pitch cost €40.

Check what the town measures before you apply. It may count canopy legs, side rails, and display tables.

Your selling width may differ from your charged width.

The most common mistake here is measuring only the front table. The Ayuntamiento may charge for the full space your stall occupies.

Three routes have different rights

A municipal fee, annual authorisation, and private rental are different arrangements. Paying one does not automatically give you the rights of another.
