Letting chilled market food sit out can make it unsafe

Source: https://weeklymarketsspain.com/letting-chilled-market-food-sit-out-can-make-it-unsafe/
Chilled market food begins to warm the moment it leaves refrigeration, creating conditions that can allow harmful bacteria to multiply. Getting groceries home and storing them promptly is a critical first step in food safety.

Follow the storage order from the door

Put frozen food away first, then chilled food, fruit and vegetables, and finally dry goods.

Perishable food should reach the fridge or freezer within two hours at room temperature, or within one hour on a very hot day; keep raw products separate from ready-to-eat foods.

Pack the market bag before you leave

Use an insulated bag to slow warming during the journey home.

Separate raw food from ready-to-eat food

Place raw meat, poultry, and fish in a sealed bag or leak-proof box, separate from cheese, cooked food, pastries, and fruit for immediate eating. Raw juices can contaminate food that will not be cooked.

Add cold packs for longer trips

Add frozen cold packs around chilled purchases for journeys of 30 to 60 minutes, especially on hot days. For longer trips, use one bag for raw meat or fish and another for dairy, cooked food, and frozen products.
