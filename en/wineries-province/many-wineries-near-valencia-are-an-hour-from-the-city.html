Choosing wineries near Valencia is easier when you plan a real inland day trip. Most visit-ready estates are in Utiel-Requena, about an hour from Valencia. The best choice depends on transport, budget, and whether you want Bobal, sparkling wine, or a long local lunch.

The most practical winery day trips from central Valencia take 70 to 90 minutes each way. Requena is the clearest first choice for Bobal, traditional-method sparkling wine, and lunch in a historic town.

A useful rule for Valencia visitors: allow 5 to 6 hours for one winery and lunch. Allow 8 to 10 hours for two visits with a driver or organised pickup. A tasting normally lasts 60 to 120 minutes.
