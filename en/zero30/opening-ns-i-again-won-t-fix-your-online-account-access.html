Opening NS&I again won’t fix your online account access

NS&I online account | NS&I registration | Premium Bonds login | Premium Bonds management | NS&I number

Source: https://zero30.uk/opening-ns-i-again-won-t-fix-your-online-account-access/
Regaining access to your NS&I online account should not require opening another savings product. Registration is designed to link eligible holdings to a single secure account without purchasing new Premium Bonds.

You have an NS&I number, perhaps from Premium Bonds bought years ago or an ISA opened on paper, and need to check a balance or update your details. Registering for online access is different from opening a new product, which could create a separate holding rather than reveal savings you already own.

Managing NS&I savings online: register first

Register an existing holding to manage eligible NS&I products. Opening a product puts money into new savings or buys Premium Bonds; registration links eligible holdings already in your name to the Online and Phone Service.

Separate the product from the login

Premium Bonds have a holder’s number for your holding and individual Prize Bond numbers for the monthly draw. Your NS&I number is the wider online-service reference, so do not assume a Prize Bond number is your sign-in reference.

Gather the details before you start

Have your NS&I number, full legal name, date of birth, postcode, email address and mobile number ready. Use the name and address NS&I already holds, as an older account may still show a previous postcode or surname and prevent an automatic match.

Not every NS&I holding follows the same registration path.
