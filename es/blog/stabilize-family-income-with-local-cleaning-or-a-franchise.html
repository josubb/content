![Stabilize family income with local cleaning or a franchise](/images/stabilize-family-income-with-local-cleaning-or-a-franchise.jpg) ## Which model fits a parent’s need for steady income and hours Independent owners keep more revenue and set hours around childcare windows. Franchises give systems and leads but add recurring fees that cut household cashflow. The decision rests on how much predictable monthly income the household needs, how many reliable weekly hours a parent can supply, and how much fixed monthly cost the family can handle. ### How to measure predictability Predictability equals recurring revenue minus fixed monthly obligations. Calculate the net monthly take-home after royalties, marketing fees, payroll, and taxes before deciding. A clear number here answers whether the business will help or hurt monthly stability.
